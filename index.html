About RainyNews

RainyNews is an independent news-style website project designed to make interesting topics easier and more enjoyable to explore.

🌧️ Why RainyNews?

Traditional news can sometimes feel overwhelming or boring. RainyNews is designed around simple explanations, interesting topics and a fun browsing experience.

🔎 Important

The stories currently included in this website are demo/editorial content rather than a live news feed. Important or breaking news should always be checked against reliable news organisations and official sources.

🚀 What's next?

RainyNews can eventually grow with live news feeds, more categories, more games, article publishing tools, weather information and much more.
